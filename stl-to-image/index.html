Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I convert STL to transparent PNG?

Select "PNG Image (Transparent Background)" in the Output Format dropdown on the right panel, then click "Convert & Download Image".

Q: Are my STL files uploaded to a server?

No. All WebGL 3D rendering and image conversions take place completely inside your local browser memory. Your 3D models remain 100% private.
